Ph.D. Candidate in Economics
Vancouver School of Economics, University of British Columbia

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at the Vancouver School of Economics, University of British Columbia. My research is in political economy and public economics: I study how anti-corruption enforcement shapes public procurement, and how economic shocks and institutions shape political polarization. Before the Ph.D., I was an Empirical Research Fellow at Northwestern University’s Kellogg School of Management and an Economics Consultant at the Inter-American Development Bank. I hold a B.A. in Economics and Political Science from ITAM.
